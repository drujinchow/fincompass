Medicine trains you for everything except the choices around it.

Which specialty. Whether to stay in public service. How to build a marriage and a family on call rosters. These decisions shape the next thirty years, and most young doctors make them alone. FinCompass gives you a thinking partner outside your assessment chain.

Who it's for

Free

Medical students

Find your why before housemanship. Talks, small-group cohorts and 1:1 coaching for final-years.

Subsidised

House officers and medical officers

Specialty choice, residency applications, bond decisions and the question of staying or leaving.

Subsidised

Young doctor families

Couples and parents building a home around training, shifts and two demanding careers.

Three programmes

One compass for the whole of your life in medicine.

Purpose & Life

Why you chose medicine, what you want from it, and how to protect the rest of your life while you train.

Career

Specialty choice, residency, public or private practice, and the paths beyond clinical medicine.

Family

Relationships, marriage and parenting through training years, for you and your partner.

How it works

01

Talks

Open sessions with Dr U-Jin Chow and guest doctors on the decisions that shape a medical life.

02

Cohorts

Small-group coaching over 6 to 8 weeks with peers facing the same crossroads.

03

1:1 coaching

Private sessions matched to a coach who fits your goals, at your pace.
